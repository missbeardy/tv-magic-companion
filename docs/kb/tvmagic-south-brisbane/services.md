# Services TV Magic South Brisbane can help with

Answer “do you do X?” from this list. If it is listed, say yes in plain language and collect their details. If it is not listed, say a technician will confirm — do not guess.

You still never quote prices.

## Antenna and reception

- Digital TV antenna installation, relocation, and removal
- Reception repair (pixelation, flickering, glitchy or missing audio, missing channels, no signal)
- Extra-high antennas and extended masts
- Manual TV tuning and setup so channels lock to the strongest signal
- MATV / commercial antenna systems (units, buildings, multi-dwelling)
- VAST satellite TV for blackspot / poor terrestrial reception

## TV installation and mounting

- Supply and installation of TVs
- Wall mounting on plaster, brick, or concrete — cables concealed where practical
- Outdoor TV hanging
- Extra TV points and outlets
- Sound bar installation and mounting
- Video walls / multi-monitor displays (homes and commercial)

## Home theatre and cinema

- Home theatre setup (THX-certified capability on the national team)
- Home cinema / media rooms — projector, screen, speakers, calibration
- Universal remotes and programming

## Satellite, internet, PayTV

- Satellite dish install, realign, relocate
- Foxtel / PayTV points (including splitting an existing subscription to another room where legal and practical)
- Starlink install (roof dish + cable to the modem)
- Christian TV channel setup after the right reception path is in place

## Other

- CCTV advice and installation (Brisbane north and south)
- Phone points and data points
- Electrical work — Brisbane only; South Brisbane can take the enquiry and a technician will confirm if they can do that job

## Common “yes” examples

- “Do you wall mount TVs?” Yes.
- “Can you fix pixelating channels?” Yes — reception repair and/or tuning.
- “Do you do Starlink?” Yes.
- “Extra TV point in the bedroom?” Yes.
- “Home theatre / soundbar / CCTV / MATV / VAST?” Yes — take the lead.
