# Franchise self-serve email templates

Visual block builder in Franchise Settings for quote, lead-ack, and invoice emails. Per-org overrides with brand → default fallback. No raw HTML for franchisees.

---

## Board review 06-08-2026 — THE MOST QUESTIONABLE CARD ON THE BOARD

A visual block builder in Franchise Settings so franchisees can edit quote, lead-ack and invoice emails without touching raw HTML.

**Three problems:**

1. **There are no franchisees.** Prod has two brands: `tv-magic` (one real org) and `fieldbourne` (a demo). Zero people would use this builder. BUSINESS.md correctly parks the white-label/franchise motion as a *second* business model, to start only after the solo motion works.
2. **It is not on ROADMAP.md.** Not Tier 1, 2 or 3. It was picked up 27-07-2026 outside the governance process that ROADMAP.md rule 1 exists to enforce — the same pattern as T3.1 Xero being built three days after T2.9 decided it stays Tier 3.
3. **It is marked in-progress at high priority**, i.e. it is currently competing with the Critical list for the only developer.

Brand and per-org email template editors **already exist** in Platform Admin. This card replaces a working founder-operated tool with a self-serve tool for users who do not exist.

**Verdict: park it.** Dropped to `low` and moved to Review. If there is a franchise prospect in the pipeline I do not know about, say so and this changes completely — that is genuinely a differentiated B2B2B asset. Absent that prospect, this is the clearest example of the review's core finding: building because it is buildable.
