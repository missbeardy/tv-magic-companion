# DD13 Get five paying strangers

**The most damning number in the repository: 1 customer, 20+ roadmap items shipped in three weeks, 0 requested by a stranger who has ever paid.**

Engineering velocity has not been the constraint on this business for a long time. This card is the one that converts BUSINESS.md and MARKETING.md from hypothesis into data — and it will invalidate a third of the roadmap for free, which is a saving, not a loss.

**Spec:**
1. **Demo to twenty solo tradies.** Sources: local trade Facebook groups, the current client's trade-adjacent network (antenna/AV → handyman, locksmith, garage doors, pest, lawn), one or two bookkeepers (they answer "what should I use?").
2. **Charge the first five.** Founding price is fine; **zero is not** — free users do not tell the truth about value. Founding discount in exchange for a review + testimonial + feedback calls, per BUSINESS.md.
3. **Onboard each personally and watch them use it.** Note every confusion, every question, every place they stop. This is the UX telemetry that doesn't exist yet (`dd1` gives the quantitative half; this is the qualitative half).
4. **Track the objections verbatim.** Especially: *"how does the booked job get into ServiceM8?"* — the front-door add-on position has no answer to this today, and it kills the pitch in month two as churn rather than month one as a lost sale.

**Also resolves the review's open questions:**
- Is the current client actually paying, and how much? (Moves the PMF score by ±3.)
- Does $69/mo AUD GST-inc survive contact with a stranger?

**Blocked by (soft):** `dd12` front-door assets make this dramatically easier — but do not let asset-building become another reason not to talk to people. Demo live from a phone if the video isn't ready.

**Done when:** five tradies who are not the current client are paying real money, each has been onboarded and observed, and their objections + confusions are written up as the input to the *next* roadmap.

**Difficulty:** Hard — and it is the only item on this board that cannot be done at a keyboard.
