# DD12 Landing page, demo video and case study

**There is no front door.** No landing page, no pricing page, no recorded demo, no case study. A tradie encountering FieldBourne today learns nothing. More strategy has been written about these assets than it would take to produce them — BUSINESS.md's "Sales assets checklist" has had five unticked boxes since 20-07-2026, each about a half-day of work.

## 1. Record the 60-second demo

T2.2 shipped a *runbook* (`docs/DEMO_RUNBOOK.md`), not an asset. The beat sheet in MARKETING.md is already written and every beat is built. Record it: screen capture + both phones, cold, on a real Android. This one video is the website hero, the social ad, and the DM answer.

## 2. Case study with the current client

BUSINESS.md calls it *"the single highest-value asset available right now"* — months of daily production use with real numbers. Ask permission, pull the stats from `lead_events` / `monthly_org_reports` (leads captured, response time, invoices paid, days-to-paid), one page + three pull-quotes.

## 3. Landing page with pricing

- Lead with the **loss**, not the software category: *"You missed 3 calls this week. Two of them booked with someone else."*
- $69/mo AUD GST-inc, messaging included (T2.9 decision).
- Missed-call cost calculator as the lead magnet (jobs/week × close rate × avg job).
- Honest one-page comparison vs ServiceM8/Tradify, front-door framing, including what they do better.
- **A signup form is fine — it does not need to be self-serve.** T3.11 stays deferred.
- Doubles as the hosting location for the privacy policy and terms from `dd3`.

## Claims discipline

Every public claim must trace to a shipped, switch-ON, verified feature. Never claim: native app, certificates, recurring jobs, offline *everything*. (Carry-over from MARKETING.md.)

**Feature switch:** none.

**Done when:** the demo video is recorded and watchable; the case study is a shareable one-pager with real numbers; the landing page is live with pricing, the calculator, and a working signup form; all three are linked from one another.

**Difficulty:** Medium — mostly non-engineering. **Unblocks `dd13`.**
